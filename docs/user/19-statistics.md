# Statistics

Choose **Statistics…** from the **☰** menu for a plain-text summary of the current log. It is counted when the window opens (a few moments on a very large log) and nothing runs in the background. **Copy** puts the whole text on the clipboard, so you can paste it into an email, a forum post or a note.

The summary has four parts:

- **Totals**: QSOs, different callsigns, and DXCC entities worked and confirmed.
- **DXCC progress by year**: for each year the QSOs made, the entities worked for the first time that year (**New**) and the running total of entities (**Total**).
- **Confirmation rates**: for LoTW, paper QSL, QRZ Logbook, Club Log and eQSL, how many QSOs were sent, how many came back confirmed, and the percentage. A confirmed QSO counts as sent even if it was never marked sent. QRZ and Club Log confirmations are shown here but don't count towards the [Awards](11-awards).
- **Most worked**: the ten entities and the ten callsigns with the most QSOs.

An entity counts as confirmed here when any of its QSOs has a LoTW or paper confirmation. The entity of each QSO is its DXCC field, or the country file when the field is blank. LoTW's entity replaces the stored one when you download confirmations, as in the Awards pane.

The statistics cover every QSO in the log, including those made with archived callsigns.
